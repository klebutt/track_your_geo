Privacy Policy

Who we are

AI Recommend helps UK businesses understand how visible they are in AI assistant answers.

Data controller: Kieran Le Butt (sole trader, trading as AI Recommend)

Contact: kieran@airecommend.co.uk

What data we use to create your report

To produce an AI visibility report for your firm, we use:

  • Your public business website – to understand your location, services, and how you describe your firm
  • Public business listings – such as Google Business Profile, where available
  • Your business email address – to contact you about the report

We do not use any personal data about individuals, only publicly available business information.

Lawful basis

We process business contact data under legitimate interest (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). Our legitimate interest is to help UK businesses understand and improve their visibility in AI assistant answers.

For B2B marketing communications, we rely on the "soft opt-in" provisions of the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), sending relevant information to business contacts who have not opted out.

How we log report page views

When you view a report page (URLs starting with /r/), we log:

  • Timestamp – when the page was viewed
  • Report slug – which report was accessed (an anonymous identifier)
  • Country – derived from Cloudflare's edge location, not your IP address
  • User agent – your browser and device type
  • ASN – the network you are connecting from (e.g., a mobile carrier or ISP)

We do not store your IP address.

This logging helps us understand when reports are viewed and filter out automated bot traffic. It is processed under legitimate interest.

Data retention

We retain report view logs for 12 months, after which they are deleted.

Business contact data (email addresses) is retained until you opt out or ask us to delete it.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about your business
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interest

To exercise these rights, email kieran@airecommend.co.uk.

How to opt out

If you do not want us to contact you:

  • Reply to any email from us with "stop"
  • Email kieran@airecommend.co.uk asking to be removed

We will remove your contact details within 7 days.

Third-party services

Cookies

Our website does not use cookies for tracking or analytics. The only cookies may be those set by Cloudflare for security and performance purposes.
