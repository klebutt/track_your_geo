Who we are

AI Recommend helps UK businesses understand how visible they are in AI assistant answers.

Data controller: Kieran Le Butt (sole trader, trading as AI Recommend)

Contact: kieran@airecommend.co.uk

What personal data we use

We only contact UK limited companies and LLPs about our service. Depending on what is publicly available and what you share with us, we may process:

  • Business contact details – such as your name, work email address, job title or role, and firm name, taken from your firm’s public website and, where we need to check a name matches the business, from Companies House
  • Public business information – your firm’s website, location, services, and public listings (for example Google Business Profile), used to prepare an AI visibility report
  • Report page visits – when someone opens a private report link (/r/…), we log technical details (see below); the link identifies the firm the report was prepared for, not you personally by name
  • Booking details – if you book a call via airecommend.co.uk/book, Google Calendar collects the information you enter (such as name and email)
  • Email correspondence – the content of emails you send us and our replies, including opt-out requests

A work email that includes your name (for example firstname@firm.co.uk) is personal data under UK GDPR, even when we use it in a business context.

Why we use it

We use this information to:

  • Prepare a one-off, free AI visibility report for your firm
  • Email you the report and send one follow-up about our paid service if we have not heard back
  • See whether report links are opened (and reduce noise from bots)
  • Arrange calls you book with us and respond to your messages
  • Honour opt-outs and keep a record so we do not contact you again

Lawful basis

We process contact and outreach data under legitimate interests (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). Our interest is to tell relevant UK professional firms about a free report and our service in a measured, targeted way. We balance this against your rights; you can object at any time (see below).

For marketing email, we contact corporate subscribers – limited companies and LLPs – at business email addresses. Under PECR, we do not need prior consent for that type of B2B email, provided we identify ourselves and offer a simple way to opt out. We do not send unsolicited marketing email to sole traders or ordinary partnerships without their consent.

Report view logs and bot filtering are also processed under legitimate interest. If you book a call, Google processes your booking under its own terms; we use booking details to run the appointment.

How we log report page views

When you view a report page (URLs starting with /r/), we log:

  • Timestamp – when the page was viewed
  • Report slug – which report was accessed (an anonymous identifier in the URL)
  • Country – derived from Cloudflare’s edge location, not your IP address
  • User agent – your browser and device type
  • ASN – the network you are connecting from (for example a mobile carrier or ISP)

We do not store your IP address in our report open log.

This logging helps us understand when reports are viewed and filter out automated bot traffic.

How long we keep data

  • Report view logs – up to 12 months, then deleted
  • Prospect contact details and outreach notes – up to 12 months after our last contact with you, unless we are in an ongoing conversation or you become a client (in which case we keep what we need for that relationship)
  • Opt-out / suppression list – kept indefinitely (usually only an email address) so we do not email you again
  • Emails – kept as long as needed for the purposes above, then deleted or anonymised unless we must keep them for legal reasons

How to object or opt out

You can object to our use of your data for outreach, or opt out of further emails, at any time:

  • Reply to any email from us with STOP (or “stop”)
  • Email kieran@airecommend.co.uk and ask us to stop or to delete your data

We will stop marketing contact promptly, add you to our suppression list, and confirm when done. We may keep your email on that list even after other prospect data is deleted.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Ask us to erase your data (subject to exceptions, such as our suppression list)
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interest

To exercise these rights, email kieran@airecommend.co.uk.

Processors and third-party services

We use trusted providers who process data on our behalf or under their own policies:

  • AgentMail – sends and receives our outreach and support email
  • Cloudflare – hosts our website (Pages), runs our report open logger (D1 database), and provides security and analytics; Cloudflare may process technical data such as IP addresses under its own privacy policy
  • Google – Calendar for bookings at /book, and Google Workspace for email and files where we use it
  • OpenAI, Perplexity, and Google – generate report content by querying AI services with your firm name and brand-neutral search questions; we do not send personal contact details to those services for report generation

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics on our site, which does not use cookies and does not track individual users.

Cookies

Our website does not use cookies for tracking or analytics. The only cookies may be those set by Cloudflare for security and performance purposes.
